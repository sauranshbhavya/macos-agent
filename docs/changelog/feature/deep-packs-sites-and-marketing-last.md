**Reader notes — read these before opening a help centre.** Each one was met on this branch and each one is a way for a page that holds a flow to look as if it holds none, which is the clean-zero family `CLAUDE.md`'s Claims and evidence section describes, arriving through a browser.
- **Try every reader before calling a site unreadable.** On `help.samcart.com`, every `javascript_tool` call timed out at 45 seconds with the renderer unresponsive, and twice the whole tab group vanished after the navigation — while `get_page_text` on the same article, one call later, returned all of it. The same freeze hit Squarespace's help-centre home page, whose article pages loaded normally. `javascript_tool`, `get_page_text` and `read_page` fail on different pages, and a site is unreadable only when they agree.
- **A reader built on paragraphs and list items returns empty headings on a site that keeps its steps in plain `div`s.** Kajabi's help pages gave back "Remove filters" and "Save filters as a Segment" with nothing beneath them. Splitting the cleaned `textContent` at every block element recovered the steps. A heading with nothing under it means change readers, not that the section is empty.
- **The mirror of that one: `get_page_text` dropped Etsy's numbered steps.** On Etsy's help articles it returned every paragraph and the sentence "To opt out of personalized advertising with an Etsy account:" with none of the six steps after it, while the block-splitting reader returned all six. So neither reader is the safe default, and a page whose prose promises a list the reading does not show is the tell.
- **Only the title check catches a renamed article.** Squarespace's own link to its contributor article, `…/articles/206537287-Inviting-a-contributor`, lands on `…/206537287-Invite-a-contributor`, titled "Invite a contributor". Same article, renamed; the URL requested and the URL landed differ, and nothing else said so. This is SONNY-463's slug trap on a second help centre, reached by following the site's own link.
- **A JS string over about a thousand characters comes back truncated** in the tool's output. Return an array of lines instead, which truncates only past 100 items. And keep a `browser_batch` to one page — a long batch timed out as a whole though each action inside it completed.
