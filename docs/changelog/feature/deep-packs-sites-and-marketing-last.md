Architectural decisions / pitfalls discovered (required, write "none" if true):
- **A publish flow can end at an upgrade prompt, and the page says so in a banner rather than in a step.** Glide's "Publishing and Sharing" and "Quick Start" pages both open their publish instructions with "Publishing apps requires a paid plan … If you're on the Free plan and try to publish, you'll see a prompt to upgrade." A flow written from the steps alone would load — its steps name Publish and nothing priced — and walk a free user to a purchase prompt. That is SONNY-506's class on a fresh site; it was not written, and Glide's other flows were not shipped for the start-page reason below. The same shape was checked on the six that shipped: Squarespace's "Add a new site to your account" section starts a trial on a page whose whole subject is that each site is billed separately, so the pack carries the page's switch-between-sites section instead.
- **Two sites have no start page a signed-out visitor can work from, and the own-domain rule is why — SONNY-510's fifth shape, twice.** Glide: `https://go.glideapps.com/` (the catalogue's sign-in URL) rendered "Could not load network resources / Retry" and no form on two loads 18 seconds each, and the marketing site's own Sign up and Get started links point at `os.glideapps.dev`, which is not on `glideapps.com`. Zoho Creator: the pack's domain is `creator.zoho.com`; `https://creator.zoho.com/` sends a signed-out visitor to `https://www.zoho.com/creator/`, a marketing page whose form is account creation — a password box, a captcha and an "I agree to the Terms of Service" checkbox — and the catalogue's own sign-in URL is on `accounts.zoho.com`, which the rule refuses. Both flows were read and are recorded on SONNY-504 ready to paste; what they need is a start page, not a reading.
- **A slug that is not the article's current one still lands, and only the title says so.** Squarespace's own page links `…/articles/206537287-Inviting-a-contributor`; it lands on `…/206537287-Invite-a-contributor`, titled "Invite a contributor" — the same article renamed, the SONNY-463 shape on a second help centre. Not shipped here, for the reason below, but it is the reading that makes the title check owed on every link, including a help centre's own.
- **A reader that returns a page's tag text misses a documentation site that puts its steps in `div`s.** On Kajabi's Mintlify pages, a reader built on `p`, `li` and headings returned the section headings and nothing under "Remove filters" or "Save filters as a Segment". Splitting the cleaned `textContent` on every block element recovered the steps. A heading with nothing beneath it is the signal to change readers, not evidence that the section is empty.
- **One help centre froze the browser rather than serving it, and a second reader got through.** Every `javascript_tool` call on `help.samcart.com` timed out at 45 seconds with the renderer unresponsive, and twice the tab group itself disappeared after the navigation. `get_page_text` on the same URL, one call later, returned the whole article. Three readers, three answers: an exclusion needs the readers to agree, and here they did not.
