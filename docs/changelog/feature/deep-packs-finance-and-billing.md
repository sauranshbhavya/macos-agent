Architectural decisions / pitfalls discovered (required, write "none" if true):
- **Two sites cannot be deep without an identity-host pairing, and that list is Swift this ticket may not touch.** QuickBooks: the pack's domain is `quickbooks.intuit.com`, the product is `qbo.intuit.com` (off that domain, so a flow cannot even start there), and signed out it lands on `accounts.intuit.com/app/sign-in`, which is neither on the pack's site nor a listed identity host. Zoho Books: `books.zoho.com/` lands on the marketing page `www.zoho.com/us/books/`, and its sign-in is `accounts.zoho.com`, also unlisted. Each needs a founder decision on SONNY-524's list, not a data edit.
- **A bot check is a start page nobody can read.** FreshBooks' `my.freshbooks.com/` lands on `auth.freshbooks.com/oauth/authorize` titled "Checking your browser – reCAPTCHA" and stayed there; a lane presses nothing on a bot check, so there is no reading to record and no pack.
- **The money rule refuses a real control name, and the flow goes rather than the name.** Wise's team-member flow runs through a control called "Team members and payment approvals", and Wave's add-a-customer flow through a menu called "Sales & Payments"; beside an "Add" or "Remove" each is `add + payment` or `remove + payment`. Both are false refusals by the rule's own admission, and renaming a control a user must find is worse than losing the flow. Wave's import flow names the same menu and loads, because it names no listed action verb, which says the rule is reading words and not the task.
- **Money sat one control away on most of these pages, and the flows stop short of it.** Wise's team-member page ends in payment approvals and card spending limits; Square's add-a-team-member steps set pay type and a passcode; Dext's add-a-user page offers "Purchase additional users" at the plan limit; Stripe's customer-portal page (the row's old `doc_url_2`) configures cancelling plans and changing cards. None is a flow seed here, and Dext's flow says to stop at the plan's user limit rather than change the plan.
- **Two help centres refuse `get_page_text`** (clockify.me and support.waveapps.com answer "Permission denied for reading page content on this domain"), and some output is withheld by the tool as query-string data. Reading the DOM through `javascript_tool`, with `?`, `=` and `&` folded to spaces in the output, works on both.
- **Machine load decides whether a page can be read at all.** At a load average of 77 to 91 (another lane's deliberate CPU burners, then Swift builds) the renderer froze on Zoho's marketing page, Razorpay's and Airwallex's sign-in pages, NetSuite's marketing site and Ramp's sign-in, and the tab group reset three times.
