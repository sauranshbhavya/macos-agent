A remembered account is not a live session, so both readings are correct; what they disagree about is whether the profile can act as the founder, and the chooser is the ingredient SONNY-510's LinkedIn One Tap case turned on. **The consequence for method:** a signed-out start-page reading taken on such a profile is not the page a fresh Mac meets — a fresh Mac has no remembered account and lands on `/signin/identifier`, which is the shape the Meet precedent recorded.
