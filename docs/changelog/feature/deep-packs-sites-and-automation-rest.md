Architectural decisions / pitfalls discovered (required, write "none" if true):
- **A start page can be account creation on every route into the product, and then the honest answer is to ship no pack at all.** Bubble's flows were read and written before its start page was opened. `https://app.bubble.io/login` — the value its catalogue row already held — redirects to `https://bubble.io/login`, whose panel reads "Sign up and start building" over an email and password form, with "By signing up, you agree to our Terms and Privacy Policy" bound to its **Start building** button and Login a second button beside it; `https://bubble.io/home` redirects to the same page. That is SONNY-510's shape 1 (a redirect to account creation) arriving on top of its shape 3 (the landed page's own primary control is the sign-up form), and no signed-out route into the Projects section was found on `bubble.io`. The pack stays shallow and the article that was read — `https://bubble.io/support/en/articles/10581123-creating-managing-projects`, title "Creating & Managing Projects | Bubble Support Center" — is recorded on the ticket so a later session does not pay for that reading twice. **Nothing in this repository would have refused it**: `app.bubble.io` is a subdomain of `bubble.io`, so the host check passes and is right to, and the URL's own text carries no credential word.
- **A flow whose last step is a subscription popup is SONNY-506's shape, and the page names no money word until you are standing on it.** Duda's "Unpublish a Site" was read in full and dropped: its fourth step is a popup asking whether to stop renewing the site subscription, with "Do Not Renew" the control the article tells you to select. Every step before it is ordinary interface work, and `SkillPackMoneyRule` reads the flow's title and steps as one unit — so a flow written from the first three steps would have loaded cleanly and walked a user to a renewal decision. Named on SONNY-504 and on SONNY-506, as the ticket asks.
- **Two of the four start pages tell you nothing on a first read, for two different reasons, and both look like "this page cannot be read".** Teachable's sign-in path answers a Cloudflare interstitial titled "Just a moment..." with **0** inputs of any kind; waiting nine seconds and reading again gives "Teachable Accounts" with the log-in form. Podia's and Duda's log-in pages ask for an email before they build the password field, so a masked-input count at load is an absence at that instant rather than a property of the page — which is SONNY-510's own measurement about Wix, met twice more here. What decided all four was the landed page's title and whether account creation was the form or a demoted link, never a field count.
- **The stale-read trap fired once, on an ordinary help-centre hop.** Navigating to Bubble's Getting Started collection and reading in the same batch returned the *previous* page's text under the previous page's URL, while the tab's own title had already changed — so the two disagreed inside one call. Every reading after that waited and read the URL from the page, and every article was read twice; the two reads agreed everywhere.
- **`get_page_text` and `javascript_tool` are denied per domain, and `read_page` is not.** JavaScript execution is refused on `help.podia.com`, `app.podia.com` and `app.bubble.io`, and page-text reading is refused on `app.bubble.io`. `read_page` answered on all of them, and its accessibility tree carries `href`, which is what let the help-centre indexes be followed by their own links rather than by guessed URLs. A lane that treats a JavaScript refusal as "this site cannot be read" loses sites it could have had.
