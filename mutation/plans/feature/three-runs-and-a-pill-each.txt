# SONNY-533 and the second layer of SONNY-456. Swift half only.
#
# S1 to S9: the "Task failed" notification's Retry re-runs the task it was posted for, and no other.
# Killers are in Tests/MacAgentTests/RunAttributedRetryTests.swift (the RunAttributedRetryTests and
# NotificationRoundTripTests suites).
# H1: a screen-control question's cancellation resumes the run that parked it. Killers are in
# Tests/MacAgentTests/RunAttributedCancellationTests.swift.
# V1 and V2: a voice recording writes to the run it started on. Killers are in
# Tests/MacAgentTests/RunAttributedVoiceTests.swift.
# C1 and K1: the cap of three, and the emergency key stopping every run. Killers are in
# Tests/MacAgentTests/ConcurrentRunTests.swift.
# G1 to G6: what deletes, and what starts unattended, reads every run and not the one on screen.
# Killers are EveryRunGuardTests in Tests/MacAgentTests/ConcurrentRunTests.swift, and for G6
# nothingFiresWhileATaskIsRunningInARunTheWidgetIsNotShowing in ScheduledRoutineRunTests.swift.
#
# Proved by hand on this branch, applied together and read per killer because their killers are
# disjoint: S2 and H1 with an earlier form of S1, then V1, C1 and K1, then G1, G5 and G6. The rest
# are written and not run; the changelog entry says which.
