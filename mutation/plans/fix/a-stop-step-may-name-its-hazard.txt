# SONNY-534 and SONNY-536 — two changes to one file family, pulling in opposite directions: 534 widens
# what the credential rule refuses, 536 carves out what a stop may say. Swift half only; no file under
# server/ changes on this branch.
#
# The mutants are written against properties, not files. M1–M8 weaken the minting test in the
# direction that lets a credential flow load, or disturb the word an older refusal has always been
# refused on. M9 widens it, and is here because "0 of the shipped texts refused" is as load-bearing a
# claim as the refusals: a credential rule that refuses working flows is one a lane switches off.
#
# S1 narrows the stop exemption back to nothing. S2–S4 WIDEN it, which is the direction SONNY-536
# names: "a mutant that widens it must die". S5–S7 remove one of the three things that hold a stop to
# being one act, S8–S9 take the instruction out of code's hands or move it below the steps, and
# S10–S12 are the shape checks' own edges.
